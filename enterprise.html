Enterprise FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Can SSH Desktop be deployed in completely air-gapped and offline enterprise environments?

Yes. SSH Desktop requires zero internet connectivity to function. It communicates purely peer-to-peer over your internal SSH2 network (port 22) and transmits zero telemetry, analytic beacons, or licensing pings to any external server.

How does SSH Desktop handle compliance requirements like SOC 2, ISO 27001, and HIPAA?

Because SSH Desktop never stores credentials on third-party cloud servers and leaves all keys encrypted in local OS keystores (Windows DPAPI, macOS Keychain, Linux Secret Service), your infrastructure meets strict zero-trust, data-residency, and compliance boundaries out of the box.

Does SSH Desktop support Jump Hosts, Bastions, and ProxyJump configurations?

Yes. Enterprise teams can seamlessly route through single or multi-tier SSH bastion jump hosts using OpenSSH-compatible ProxyJump and ProxyCommand directives, ensuring private VPC and DMZ servers are never directly exposed to the internet.

Are there enterprise licensing fees, seat limits, or paid add-ons?

No. SSH Desktop is 100% free under the permissive Apache 2.0 and MIT open-source licenses. There are zero artificial user caps, zero tier gating, and no commercial licensing fees for enterprise fleets.
