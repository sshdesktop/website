FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What Linux distributions are supported by the firewall manager?

SSH Desktop supports all standard Linux distributions including Ubuntu 20.04/22.04/24.04 LTS, Debian 11/12, CentOS/RHEL 8/9, Rocky Linux, AlmaLinux, Fedora, and Arch Linux.

Can I delete or reorder firewall rules?

Yes. You can delete rules with a single click or insert new rules at specific numerical positions (e.g., ufw insert 1 allow ...) to maintain strict rule evaluation order.

Does it support IPv6 rules?

Yes. Rules are applied across both IPv4 and IPv6 tables simultaneously when IPv6 is enabled in your server configuration.
