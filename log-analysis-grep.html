FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I search compressed log archives (.gz)?

Yes. The studio seamlessly invokes streaming zgrep under the hood to search rotated gzipped archives without decompressing them to disk, saving space and time.

How does SSH Desktop handle multi-gigabyte log files without freezing?

It reads files via reverse byte-offset seek buffers rather than reading the entire file into memory. It streams only the newest requested chunk (e.g. last 1,000 lines) and lazily fetches older lines on scroll-up.

Can I export filtered log snippets for bug reports?

Yes. A single "Copy Filtered Lines" or "Save to File" button exports matching log entries directly to your local clipboard or downloads it as a .log or .txt file.
