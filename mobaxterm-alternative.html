FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I import my MobaXterm saved sessions into SSH Desktop?

In MobaXterm, export your sessions to an .ini or .mxtsessions file. In SSH Desktop, click Settings > Import Sessions > MobaXterm. Your hostnames, ports, usernames, and SSH key paths will be automatically converted and securely imported into your local encrypted vault.

Can I split terminal panes horizontally and vertically?

Yes. Just like MobaXterm, SSH Desktop supports multi-tab sessions and multi-pane splitting (Ctrl+Shift+D for vertical split, Ctrl+Shift+E for horizontal split) with independent PTY shells.

Does SSH Desktop support X11 forwarding?

Yes. You can enable X11 forwarding with a single toggle in your host profile. However, SSH Desktop's 25 native graphical applications (file manager, code editor, database studio, Docker hub, firewall) render natively with zero latency, making slow X11 forwarding obsolete for 99% of tasks.
