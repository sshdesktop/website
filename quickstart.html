SSH Desktop 3-Minute Quickstart Guide

From zero to visual server desktop in 3 minutes. Connect to any Linux VPS, import your existing SSH keys, and experience macOS-style server management.

1

Step 1: Download & Launch Native Binary

Download the pre-compiled installer for your operating system: macOS (.dmg), Windows (.msi), or Linux (.AppImage). Installation takes under 15 seconds.

2

Step 2: Add or Auto-Import Your Server

Click + New Connection. Enter your server hostname or IP address, port 22, and select your private key (Ed25519 / RSA) or enter your password. Existing ~/.ssh/config hosts are detected automatically.

3

Step 3: Launch Any App from the macOS Dock

Once connected, you will see the macOS Sonoma cloud desktop. Click any icon in the Dock or press Cmd/Ctrl + K to launch Terminal, Docker, Files, Databases, or Hardware Telemetry.
